Take the ingredients according to the amount of fish you have.

## Preparing the fish

Clean the fish, cut it into small pieces and debone it — remove all the small bones.

Marinate the fish with turmeric, chilli powder and salt. Add these according to the quantity of fish, and taste to check that it is well seasoned.

Fry the fish to a medium level, so that it does not turn hard and black.

## Grinding the mustard and fenugreek

Take the mustard and fenugreek in a chuvaddu kattiyulla chatti and fry them without oil. The mustard should not become black — as soon as it starts popping, take it off the fire and grind it in a mixie.

## Making the achar

If the fish was fried in a chatti and there is oil left in it, you can use the same chatti to make the achar.

Pour a little more oil into the chatti. Add enough mustard and let it start popping. Add the curry leaves, then the green chillies, then the crushed garlic and ginger. Do not use paste — it burns easily — but make sure the ginger and garlic are properly crushed with no big pieces. Mix properly and wait until the water and the raw smell of the ginger and garlic disappear.

Bring the flame down to really low. Add turmeric powder and chilli powder as needed. Once the raw smell disappears, add salt for the gravy only — remember that the fish already has salt, so add salt accordingly.

Then add the fish to the gravy and mix the fish and the masala well. Then add vinegar.

Let the mixture boil, then taste it to see whether the vinegar and salt are enough. If not, add as needed.

Last, add the ground mustard and fenugreek made earlier. Mix properly and let it cook for two minutes, then turn off the stove. Let it cool and the achar is ready.
