> Note: the quantities are on the larger side because the stew is meant to be separated into two. If you cook it as a single dish, adjust the ingredients to taste.

## Preparation

Chop the potatoes and carrots into medium-sized pieces and slice the onions very thin. Chop the ginger and garlic into very tiny pieces.

Cut the chicken into small pieces — not too small. Boil the chicken in a separate pot with turmeric, pepper and salt. Separately boil the potatoes and carrots with the same turmeric, pepper and salt.

## Method

Take a pan, pour in some oil and add the sliced onions. Cook the onions until they turn pinkish and become soft.

Add the ginger and garlic. Let it cook for two minutes, until the raw smell of the ginger and garlic goes. Add the chillies.
