## Method

Take your cut of chicken (preferably leg or thigh) and cut some slits in them.
Using the ginger garlic paste, salt and chilli powder, coat the chicken and set aside.

Cut the onion into small cubes (slices work fine too) and place in a heated and oiled pot.
Once the onions turn translucent, add salt, turmeric, coriander powder. Fry the spices, after which you can add the garlic. Add coriander after.

Once aromatised, add the chicken and let it fry for a bit. Once the natural liquids have run out, add the chopped tomatoes. Once thickened, add water as needed and allow to simmer for 30–45 minutes.
